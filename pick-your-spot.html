    Facilitator guide

    Before the session

    1. Open this file in Microsoft Edge or Google Chrome on the presenting laptop. No internet, sign-in or install needed.
    2. Connect to the big screen and press F (or Full screen).
    3. Choose Print room signs and tape each sign in its spot. The top of the screen is the front of the room (by the screen), and the bottom is the back.
    4. Open Edit questions to tailor the list. Untick any you want to skip. 10–12 questions suits a 30–40 minute session.
    5. Optional: switch on the countdown timer in Settings or with the Timer button.

    Running the game

    1. Read the question aloud. People walk to the spot that matches their answer.
    2. Once everyone has moved, select Reveal discussion (D). Give groups 1–2 minutes to chat.
    3. Select Next (→) to continue, or Previous (←) to go back.
    4. Reset returns to question 1 and the welcome screen. Your questions and settings are kept.
    Using a presentation clicker? Its forward button reveals the discussion prompt first, then moves to the next question.

    Editing questions

    • Add, edit, duplicate, delete, include or exclude questions. Reorder by dragging the ⋮⋮ handle or using the arrow buttons.
    • Each question needs 2–8 answers. Pick a spot for each answer. If the spot is taken, the two answers swap.
    • Auto-arrange spreads answers evenly around the room.
    • Changes save automatically in this browser on this computer.
    • To use your questions on another laptop, choose Export, copy the .json file across, and then choose Import.

    Privacy

    No sign-in, phones or personal data. Nothing leaves this computer.
